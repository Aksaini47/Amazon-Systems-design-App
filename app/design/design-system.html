Feature 5 · visual direction reference

Neumorphic-cum-glassmorphic + liquid glass

A clickable catalog of RF Logger's target visual language — a near-black canvas with translucent frosted surfaces, real backdrop blur, soft wide lift-shadows, and a specular highlight arc along the top edge of rounded shapes, as if light were catching curved glass. Every token below is copied from the app's real source; sections marked new are proposals this file introduces for Feature 6 to wire into Flutter.
